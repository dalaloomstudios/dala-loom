Privacy policy

How QRda handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Information we collect

QRda collects only the limited information necessary for the app to work correctly:

  • User identifiers. When a QR code is scanned, an identifier—such as an employee number used to sign in—is sent to our backend to verify the scan.
  • Camera access. The app needs access to the device camera exclusively to scan QR codes. QRda does not collect, store, or transmit photos or videos from the device.

How information is used

Information is used to verify QR-code scans and provide the service to the organization using QRda. Only the essential information needed for this process is transmitted.

Sharing

QRda does not sell or share personal information with third-party companies for marketing or advertising. Information is transmitted only to our backend servers to facilitate verification.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information transmitted by the app from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access. This includes secure communication protocols when the app communicates with our servers.

Retention and deletion

User data is managed by the administrator of the organization that uses QRda and is retained as long as necessary to provide the service. To request deletion, please contact your organization’s administrator.

Children’s privacy

QRda is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we do, we will post the new policy on this page. Please review it periodically for changes.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact our privacy team.
